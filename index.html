About the Chapter

The Youngstown chapter of the Zionist Organization of America works to strengthen support for Israel and the Jewish people in our community. We educate the public, fight antisemitism, support pro-Israel advocacy, and promote the Jewish people’s right to live and settle in their historic homeland.

Our Focus

  • Local education and community events
  • Advocacy and action alerts
  • Support for campus and student activism
  • Standing against antisemitism and anti-Israel bias
